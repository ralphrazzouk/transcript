# SPRING 2026

## MA 595QI - Finite Tensor Categories and Quantum Invariants

In this course, we went over finite tensor categories, rigidity and duals, pivotal and spherical categories, traces and dimensions, the Drinfeld center, Hopf algebras and their representations, quantum groups, link invariants (Jones polynomial, HOMFLY polynomial), and 3-manifold invariants.

## MA 595TGA - The Topology, Geometry, and Algebra of Loop Spaces

In this course, we went over the topology, geometry, and algebra of loop spaces, including the loop space of a manifold, path spaces, the free loop space, string topology operations, Hochschild and cyclic homology, iterated integrals, Chen's iterated integral map, connections to physics (sigma models, string theory), and homological algebra of loop spaces.

## PHYS 675 - General Relativity

In this course, we went over the foundations of general relativity, including differential geometry (manifolds, metric tensor, covariant derivatives, curvature, geodesics), equivalence principle, Einstein field equations, Schwarzschild solution and black holes, gravitational waves, cosmology (Friedmann-Robertson-Walker universe, cosmological parameters), and experimental tests of general relativity.
